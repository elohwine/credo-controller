# Cleaned Meeting Notes

## What LG said
- SGK Construction is a maintenance/construction company.
- Client jobs come in by email or purchase order.
- A purchase order is used to create a job card.
- The job card is assigned to operatives.
- The team takes before-and-after photos and attaches material receipts.
- Once the job is complete, the office invoices the client and clears the invoice after payment.
- The current system in use is **My Mobile Worker**.
- LG said the picture section is important and that **Site Audit Pro** has better picture quality than My Mobile Worker.
- LG explained that the key fields are the store number / PO number, job description, problem type, and attachments.
- LG also said completed, arrived, and failed jobs should be visible through color coding, and old unassigned jobs should be easy to spot.

## What Carly said
- Carly suggested integrating emails into the system.
- She wanted job cards to be linked to emails and documents so there is a full chain of history.
- She said the field app should behave more like Site Audit Pro so everything is in one place.
- She also mentioned that the system she used before, **Fuse Metrics**, had everything integrated in one platform.

## What Elowine said
- Elowine said the platform should track incoming requests, extract details from emails, and assign tasks to approved users.
- He said the system should track progress, status, and evidence at each stage.
- He suggested packaging evidence such as images or geolocation and attaching it to invoices.
- He said payments should be linked back to the invoice, job card, and audit trail.
- He also said the dashboard should show active, completed, pending, and on-hold jobs.
- He recommended an auto-reconciliation dashboard to match invoices and receipts.
- He added that the platform should be flexible enough to support multiple workflows in the future.

## What Kelvin said
- Kelvin said the goal is to combine different functions into one system instead of using separate tools.
- He described the project as something the team should present progress on in weekly meetings.
- He said the team should be ready to explain who is doing what and what stage the work is at.
- He also said the client may join future weekly meetings.

## Practical system requirements from the meeting
- Email-to-job-card flow
- Job assignment
- Field app for operatives
- Before/after photo capture
- Receipt uploads
- Invoice creation after completion
- Payment tracking and reconciliation
- Dashboard with job status
- Color-coded job states
- Audit trail/history
- Future-ready modular workflow structure
